## Contact form

If you use the contact form, the data you enter – name, email address, date and
place of the celebration, type of event and your message – is forwarded to me by
email, processed through the Web3Forms service. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b)
GDPR (steps prior to entering a contract) and your consent under Art. 6(1)(a)
GDPR. The data is used only to answer your enquiry and deleted once it is no
longer needed.

## Spam protection

Cloudflare Turnstile protects the form against automated messages. Technical
data such as your IP address is transmitted to Cloudflare on the basis of the
legitimate interest in a working form (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
